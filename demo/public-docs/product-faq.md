# Acme Widgets: public FAQ

**What does Acme make?** Widgets, in three sizes: small, medium and large.

**How long is the warranty?** Two years from the date of purchase.

**Where can I buy them?** From our web shop, or from any of our resellers in Finland and Sweden.

**How do I contact support?** support@acme.example, weekdays 9-17.
